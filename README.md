# Ceremony Studio

A planning tool for stadium ceremonies and kickoff shows: brief, concept, show design with three tiers per element, budget, production lead times, a CO2e calculator and a storyboard.

Open it at **https://chhavisdesk-web.github.io/ceremony-studio/**

- Runs entirely in your browser. Plans are saved in your own browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
- Includes an illustrative example plan (Madrid). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by UEFA, and costs and emissions are estimates.
- Emission factors are approximate and editable on the Sustainability step. Use current published factors and metered data for any formal reporting.
